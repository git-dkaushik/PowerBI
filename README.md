# PowerBI
## Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard

Power BI dashboard analyzing Blinkit sales across outlets, item categories, and locations.

Key Metrics


Total Sales: 1.20M
Avg Sales: 141
Avg Rating: 3.9
Items: 8,523


Features


Filters: Outlet Location Type, Outlet Size, Item Type
Fat Content: Low Fat vs Regular, by sales and outlet tier
Item Type: Sales ranked by category, toggle across Total Sales / Avg Sales / No. of Items / Avg Rating
Outlet Establishment Trend: Sales by year (2012–2022)
Sales by Outlet Size & Location: Tier 1/2/3 breakdown
Outlet Type Table: Total Sales, Avg Sales, Avg Rating, Item Visibility by outlet type


## Sales Performance Dashboard

Power BI dashboard analyzing U.S. sales performance by segment, category, region, and time.

Key Metrics


Sum of Sales: 2.30M
Sum of Profit: 286K
Sum of Discount: 1.56K
Sum of Quantity: 38K


Features


Sales by Segment: Consumer, Corporate, Home Office breakdown
Sales Trend: Year, quarter, month, and day view (2019–2021)
Sub-Category Funnel: Sales ranked from Phones down to Fasteners
State Map: Sales distribution across U.S. locations
Regional Sales: West, East, Central, South breakdown


Tools

Power BI Desktop, Power Query, DAX
